**Location:** Madrid, Spain

Scaling B2B SaaS for solar energy design from €230k to €2.5M ARR while growing team from 2 to 30+ engineers.

**What I did:**
- Defined roadmap focused on enterprise features and international expansion to European and LATAM markets
